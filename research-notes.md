## القمح السوري
- FAO, *Fertilizer use by crop in the Syrian Arab Republic*, Rome, 2003. النص: https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/cfefaba6-4ea4-4495-8c25-ea2fc0caa2db/content . جدول 6 عنوانه المعدلات الموصى بها حسب المحصول، بالكيلوغرام/هكتار، ويتناول فئات القمح المروي والبعلي وصنف محسن/محلي ومنطقة زراعية مرقمة. القيم في الجدول: مروي 150 N و100 فوسفات؛ بعلي محسن-1: 100 و80، محسن-2: 80 و60؛ محلي-1: 80 و60، محلي-2: 60 و60، محلي-3: 30 و30. يوضح التقرير أن P وK في هذه الدراسة معبّران بصيغة الأكاسيد؛ فالفوسفات هو P₂O₅. الجدول لا يعرض K. هذه مرجعية تاريخية لا توصية راهنة.
- ICARDA/CGIAR, Mazid et al., *Impact of Modern Agricultural Technologies on Durum Wheat Production in Syria*, report 3, 2003; technical package data include 1989 sources, and some rainfed categories differ from FAO 2003. https://mel.cgiar.org/reporting/download/hash/YhMj6HXU . تستعمل الصفحة هذا الاختلاف فقط للتحذير من انتقاء رقم واحد/دمج مراجع مختلفة، ولا تحوّل مناطق التقرير إلى محافظات اليوم.
- FAO Chapter 4 source: https://www.fao.org/4/Y4732E/y4732e08.htm . The report states rates vary according to water regime and variety, and rainfed nitrogen strategy depends on seasonal circumstances. No present-day national standard was confirmed in the reviewed sources.

## القطن السوري
- El-Haj Omar et al., *The Effect of Adding Different Levels of Fertilizer Recommendations on some Morphological and Productive Traits of Cotton (Var: Aleppo 118) in Aleppo Governorate*, Syrian Journal of Agricultural Research, received 2025-07-13, accepted 2025-09-07, issue 13(2), posted 2026. https://agri-research-journal.net/SjarEn/?p=10947 . Field trial at Tal Hadya Research Station, 2022–2023, Aleppo 118, drip irrigation, deep clay soil. Reducing the fertilizer recommendation to 75% reduced yield; reducing N to 50% affected several traits; raising N above the recommendation did not improve several traits. Site/cultivar conditions are narrow; page does not publish an independently transferable current rate in the displayed abstract.
- Janat (2008), *Response of Cotton to Irrigation Methods and Nitrogen Fertilization: Yield Components, Water-Use Efficiency, Nitrogen Uptake, and Recovery*, Communications in Soil Science and Plant Analysis 39(15–16), pp. 2282–2302. https://doi.org/10.1080/00103620802292293 . Two-season northern Syria trial tested 50, 100, 150, 200 and 250 kg N/ha under drip fertigation and furrow. The paper abstract reports 100–150 kg N/ha under drip adequate and comparable to the highest furrow rates in those trials; this is experimental context, not a general Syrian recommendation.

## الفستق الحلبي السوري
- Al-daems et al., *The Effects of Pistachio Wastes Compost in the Production of Pistachios Var. ‘Ashouri’*, Syrian Journal of Agricultural Research. Received 2024-10-31, accepted 2024-11-27. https://agri-research-journal.net/SjarEn/?p=8934 . Hamah Center of Scientific Agriculture Research, 2019–2020, Ashouri trees age 33 years. Treatments included 15 kg/tree pistachio-waste compost, mixture 7.5 kg/tree compost + 15 kg/tree organic fertilizer, and 30 kg/tree organic fertilizer, compared with farmer NPK and control. These are material quantities in one mature orchard, not nutrient-element rates for all orchards. Article's interpretation was about that experiment; avoid transferring to young orchards without soil/material analysis.
- Pistachio nutrition teaching page, UC Davis (California context): https://fruitsandnuts.ucdavis.edu/nutrients-fertilization . It emphasizes orchard-specific programs, soil analysis at establishment and leaf analysis monitoring; its California calendar/rates are not applied as Syrian rates.

## Use note
All crop rates or amounts displayed in this site are explicitly tagged with source year, experimental/zone context, nutrient basis and units. No product conversion or current universal dose is calculated. Current farm decisions require representative soil and irrigation-water tests, crop stage/cultivar and locally current extension guidance; pistachio orchard leaf or amendment analysis can be added as appropriate.
