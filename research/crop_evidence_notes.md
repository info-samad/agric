# Evidence for Syria crop directory

- FAO 2021 Crop and Food Supply Assessment Mission, December 2021: https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/45a26bb7-168c-497d-b548-cf8120c35580/content . Other crops section pp. 43–49 explicitly names lentils, chickpeas, broad beans, peas, alfalfa, forage maize, cauliflower, cabbage, tomato, cucumber, pepper, eggplant, okra, beans, zucchini, potatoes, yellow maize, cotton, tobacco, sunflower, walnut, apricot, almond, apple, citrus, grape and olive. The greenhouse subsection explicitly includes tomato, cucumbers, eggplants, chili peppers, zucchini, beans and leafy vegetables. Home garden paragraph explicitly includes onions, garlic, lettuce and cauliflower. Fruit subsection explicitly includes cherry, pistachio and pomegranate. Agroecological zones mention melon and watermelon. No contemporary rank claims are needed.
- FAO/WFP 2019 CFSAM: https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2017140/ca5934en.pdf . Food-legume area tables list lentils, chickpeas, broad beans and peas. Confirms crop categories, potatoes, vegetables and fruit trees; not a source of present-day rankings.
- FAO, Economics of Main Sub-Sectors in Syrian Agriculture, chapter 6: https://www.fao.org/4/y4890e/y4890e0e.htm . Six historical strategic crops include wheat, barley, lentils, chickpeas, cotton and sugar beet. Wheat explicitly rainfed and irrigated; cotton irrigated. Historical coverage only.
- FAO, Farming Systems of the Syrian Arab Republic, 2006: https://www.fao.org/4/ag418e/ag418e00.pdf . Describes coastal irrigated greenhouse and citrus, mountainous olive/apple/cherry systems and southern orchards with apple/grape/pear. Greenhouse discussion cautions about chemical use and nitrate leaching. Historical system coverage, not present-day ranking.
- FAO, Integration of cereal-livestock production in farming systems: https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/42a0657b-1c44-4d6f-b992-e60355293103/content/x5488e0f.htm . Explicit actual cultivation of Vicia sativa and V. ervilia in northern Syria. Supports vetch occurrence and rainfed cereal-legume forage systems; quoted historic numerical recommendations are deliberately not reused.
- FAO/FAOSTAT via HDX, Syrian Arab Republic Crops and Livestock: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/syr-faostat-crops-livestock-production . Production CSV downloaded to faostat_syria_production.csv. Dataset period 1961–2024; contributor FAO. Filtered element Production, unit t, positive values (not trade). Confirms apple, apricot, cherry, fig, pear, pistachio, plum, quince, walnut, almond, grape, olive, citrus and vegetable categories. Peaches and nectarines is one aggregated item; pumpkins/squash/gourds and cauliflower/broccoli are also aggregated items. These do not provide independent species quantities. Pomegranate and alfalfa/vetch are taken from textual FAO sources, not inferred from residual categories.

Focus/checks will be conservative qualitative prompts, not crop fertilizer programs. Source IDs support occurrence and documented production systems, not site-specific nutrient requirements. No doses, calendars or numerical thresholds will be included.
